# Tyler Flar

CS master's student at UC San Diego, researching autonomous surgical robotics at the [Advanced Robotics and Controls Lab](https://ucsdarclab.com/) and working with AquaMesh and the Scripps COOL Lab on AquaSpectra, an in-water optical water-quality sensor. In May 2026 I founded Lycanworks, a VRChat avatar studio, and I run it end to end, from the paperwork to the Python tooling behind its releases. I also build infrastructure for agents — [Tasque](https://github.com/TylerFlar/tasque2.0), a local-first daemon that runs long-horizon coding-agent work as durable jobs, and [Jackdaw](https://github.com/TylerFlar/jackdaw-balatro), a bit-exact Balatro simulator for reinforcement learning. I spent summer 2026 at Brightree (a ResMed company) building a docs-as-code toolchain for its customer-facing API.

Projects, classes, and a full CV live at [tylerflar.com](https://tylerflar.com).
